# UI review 001: iTop Wrap wireframes (2026-11-10)

Scope: all 14 artboards on the [wireframe canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/8vxVWBAftyJki24b7MDHuk). Main window (My tickets, CMDB, Organizations, People), Menu bar, Sign in, sheets (Assign, Resolve, New ticket, Set to pending), the Dropdown component, List states, and App icon concepts.

Method: code inspection of every artboard file, plus scripted scans for em dashes, placeholder links, focus styles, controls with no behaviour, and colour use. **Not rendered:** the canvas needs a claude.ai sign-in that the review browser doesn't have, so nothing was clicked. Every "PASS" below comes from reading code, not from use.

antislop mode: during (chosen earlier in this session). Direction: DESIGN.md, ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

## What passes (by code inspection)

- **R-02:** no em dashes in any artboard.
- **R-25:** every text and background pair comes from the contrast-checked tokens in DESIGN.md: teal 6.2:1, dark teal 6.82:1, red 5.7:1 and 5.97:1, secondary grey on tints 4.9:1 or more.
- **R-27:** list empty, loading and error states exist, plus empty log, "no match" in dropdowns and filters, and "none of your tickets are for Org A".
- **R-34:** every artboard has a working Dark tweak using paired tokens.
- **R-39:** icon-only buttons have `aria-label` and a tooltip; fields have visible labels; ⓘ hints are buttons with accessible names; the spinner stops under reduced motion.
- **R-32 focus:** every file that strips button styles restores a visible focus ring with `!important`.
- **R-23:** no invented logo or photos. People are initials avatars, and the app icon was picked by you from concepts.
- **R-04:** icons are line drawings standing in for SF Symbols; each one names its meaning in a tooltip.
- **Liveliness:** the dials are declared and held, with uniform rhythm on purpose. There's one focal point per screen, one accent (teal, "waiting on you"), and an identity motif (refs and times in tabular mono).
- **R-03:** not applicable as written, since this is a macOS window app rather than a website. Panes wrap instead of overflowing when the window narrows.

## Pass 2 (same day): dark mode page and full click-through

### Dark mode
- **New canvas page "Dark mode":** a dark copy of all 12 screens, each loading the light artboard with Dark switched on, so the two pages can't drift apart. Switch pages from the page menu at the top left of the canvas.
- **Found and fixed:**
  - The linked CI and other plain links used a fixed dark teal (`#0F6E56`, 2.69:1 on the dark background, failing R-25). They now use the mode-aware teal (`#9FE1CB` in dark).
  - The focus ring used the same dark teal, so it nearly disappeared in dark mode. Every focus ring now uses `#1D9E75`, which passes 3:1 in both modes (3.39:1 on white, 4.92:1 on `#1E1E1E`).
  - White specks showed at the rounded corners of dark Sign in. The wrapper background now matches.

### Click-through (rendered, signed in)

- **Dark Set to pending:** empty → red border plus "Enter why the ticket is waiting."; with a reason → "R-000412 is pending. The timer stopped and saved 0:12."
- **Dark menu bar:** starting R-000405's timer stops R-000412's and saves its time (1h 37m), and the status item resets to 0:00. Org filter → choosing Org C narrows to R-000398, the button reads "Org C", and a click outside closes the menu.
- **Dark Organizations:** Org B → its 2 tickets; Assign to me on I-000088 → moves to Assigned as "YN You". The sidebar People link → People.
- **People (light):** Caller 1 opens on "Reported by them 1"; "Assigned to them 0" → "Caller 1 has no open tickets assigned. Callers usually don't."
- **Dark Main, CMDB, Resolve, New ticket, List states, App icon:** checked visually at canvas zoom, all render with dark tokens. Their interactions were clicked in light mode in pass 1, and the dark copies run the same code.

### Found and fixed in pass 2
- **Resolve and Set to pending:** the error line appeared and disappeared above the buttons, so clearing the error made the confirm button jump out from under the cursor (it caused a misclick in testing). The error line now has reserved space, so the buttons don't move.

### Canvas preview quirk (not a design issue)
In the dark copies, the first click after a change was sometimes ignored, and a second click worked. It never happened on the light artboards, so it comes from the canvas nesting one artboard inside another, not from the design.

### UX issues for the next phase
1. **Filtering hides the running timer:** in the menu bar, filtering to an org without the running ticket hides that ticket. A running timer should stay pinned at the top whatever the filter.
2. **Unassigned tickets in the menu bar:** they have no "Assign to me" there, unlike the main window and Organizations.
3. **Escape and sheets:** Escape closing sheets can't be shown on the canvas (the canvas takes Escape). It needs checking in the real app.
